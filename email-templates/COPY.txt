NERVEXUS — EMAIL COPY
=====================
Every line of text the system can send. Edit the wording, keep the {placeholders},
send it back. Anything in {braces} is filled in at send time.

Words like mission/missions, item/items, day/days pluralise automatically — write
them singular and the code handles the "s".


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE WRAPPER  — appears on every email, around whatever sections are included
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
GREETING              Morning {name},
GREETING (no name)    Hello,
STRAPLINE             Here is everything outstanding, in one place.
BUTTON                OPEN NERVEXUS
SIGN-OFF              Best,
                      Ultra X management team
FOOTER                Turn these off in Settings → Notifications.

SUBJECT (one thing)   {name} — {the lead item's subject, from below}
SUBJECT (several)     {name} — {the lead item's subject} (+{count} more)
SUBJECT (nothing)     {name} — Nothing outstanding


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SECTION HEADINGS  — the five groups items are sorted into
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Performance Terminal
Tasks & Missions
Calendar
Logs
App Update
Other                 (only appears if something is mis-filed — a bug, not a feature)


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TASKS & MISSIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT (missions)    {name}, you have {count} mission pending.
SUBJECT (checklists)  {name}, you have {count} checklist item pending.
LINE (missions)       {names} — you have {count} mission pending completion.
LINE (one checklist)  {count} checklist item open on {title} ({open}).
LINE (several)        {count} checklist items open across {n} checklists: {title} ({open}), {title} ({open}).


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CALENDAR  — sent 24 hours ahead
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT               Event tomorrow: {title}
LINE                  {name}, you have {title} at {time} tomorrow.
SMALL LINE UNDER IT   {time} tomorrow


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PERFORMANCE TERMINAL — same-day nudge, sent 21:30 UK for the 22:00 deadline
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT               {name}, today's performance check-in is pending
LINE                  It's 9:30pm UK and today's mandatory Performance Terminal
                      check-in still hasn't been done. You have until 10pm to log
                      it and keep your streak clean.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PERFORMANCE TERMINAL — missed a day
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT               Performance Terminal — missed check-in ({streak}/7)
LINE                  Yesterday's mandatory check-in wasn't logged. This is
                      {streak} of 7 consecutive misses — the account locks at 7,
                      pending appeal. Log today's to reset the streak.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PERFORMANCE TERMINAL — account locked (the most severe email sent)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT               Performance Terminal — account locked
LINE                  Your account has been locked after 7 consecutive missed
                      daily check-ins. Log in to submit an appeal for the owner
                      to review.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. APP UPDATE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT               Nervexus {version} is out
LINE                  A new version is live. Open the app and check What's New in
                      Settings for details.
SMALL LINE UNDER IT   {version}


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. LOGS — nobody has opened the app in a while
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT               Haven't seen you in a few days
LINE                  It's been {days} days since you last opened Nervexus.
                      Nothing has been logged in that time.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. TEST EMAIL — the "Send test email" button in Settings
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT               Test reminder from Nervexus
LINE                  This is a test email — your reminder emails are working.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. YOUR OWN OVERRIDE — Settings → Notifications → Email template
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two fields in the app that override the subject and body of every email.
Empty means "use the wording above". They accept:

  {{title}}     the subject that would have been used
  {{message}}   the whole body

DEFAULT SUBJECT       {{title}}
DEFAULT BODY          {{message}}

Note: filling these in switches the email to plain text — your wording is the
point, so the designed HTML version steps aside rather than ignoring you.
