NERVEXUS — EMAIL COPY  (as of v11.228)
======================================
Every line of text the system can send. {placeholders} are filled in at send time.

THE WRAPPER — on every email
  GREETING     Good morning / Good afternoon / Good evening {name},
               (from the hour in YOUR timezone, not the server's)
  SIGN-OFF     Thank you,
               Ultra X Management team
  BUTTON       OPEN NERVEXUS
  FOOTER       Turn these off in Settings → Notifications.
  SUBJECT      {the most urgent item's subject} (+{count} more)
               The name is added only if that subject does not already contain it.

SECTION HEADINGS
  Performance Terminal
  Daily Tasks          (was two sections: Daily Checklists + Tasks & Missions)
  Calendar
  Logs
  App Update

DAILY TASKS
  SUBJECT  Hey {name}, you have {count} tasks pending completion
  BODY     You have {count} tasks pending, awaiting completion. You have {hours}
           hours left until the day ends. Please log in as soon as possible.

PERFORMANCE TERMINAL — 21:00 UK, one hour before the deadline
  SUBJECT  Good evening {name} — your Daily Performance log is due
  BODY     It's 9pm UK time — in one hour you will need to fill out a mandatory
           Daily Performance log. This ensures that you are keeping yourself to a
           high standard. Failing this will lead to a strike on your account.

PERFORMANCE TERMINAL — a strike
  SUBJECT  Performance Terminal — strike {n} of 7
  BODY     Yesterday's mandatory Daily Performance log wasn't filled out, so a
           strike has been added to your account. This is strike {n} of 7 — the
           account locks at 7, pending appeal. Log today's to clear the strikes.

PERFORMANCE TERMINAL — locked
  SUBJECT  Performance Terminal — account locked
  BODY     Your account has been locked after 7 strikes. Log in to submit an
           appeal for the owner to review.

CALENDAR — 24 hours ahead
  SUBJECT  Tomorrow: {title}
  BODY     For tomorrow you have {title} at {time}, which you have set for
           yourself to do.
  SMALL    {time} tomorrow

  NOT YET POSSIBLE — the work-event and combined wordings need end_time,
  attendees and a work/general flag on the events table. The migration is
  written (supabase/migrations/20260901_event_details.sql); the calendar form
  still needs the fields before the other two sentences can be used.

LOGS — same 21:00 trigger as the performance log
  SUBJECT  Good evening {name} — your logs are pending
  BODY     Your logs are pending. You have {list} still to fill in. You can do
           all of them in one place — open the app and tap "Log everything".
  SMALL    {n} of 5 outstanding

  Only the MISSING logs are named. The five checked: Training, Finance,
  Sleep & energy, Hydration, Body metrics.
  Energy is a field on the sleep row, not a separate log, so it is counted once.
  Nutrition is not checked: the `meals` table has no date column, so there is no
  way to ask whether one was logged today.

APP UPDATE
  SUBJECT  Hey {name} — Nervexus {version} is out
  BODY     A new version is live. Open the app and check What's New in Settings
           for details.
  SMALL    {version}

LOGS — been away a while
  SUBJECT  Haven't seen you in a few days
  BODY     It's been {days} days since you last opened Nervexus. Nothing has been
           logged in that time.

TEST EMAIL — the "Send test email" button
  SUBJECT  Test reminder from Nervexus
  BODY     This is a test email — your reminder emails are working.

YOUR OWN OVERRIDE — Settings → Notifications → Email template
  Accepts {{title}} and {{message}}. Empty means "use the copy above".
  Filling it in switches the email to plain text.
