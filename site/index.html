◆ HOW IT WORKS
Private. Daily. Genuinely intelligent.
From getting in, to living in it every day, to the AI actually behind each part.
1

Request access

This is a private platform — you'll need to be accepted to join. There's no open sign-up; every account is reviewed before it's created.

2

Built into your day

Log your training before breakfast, check the budget at lunch, run a learning module in the evening, ask for a briefing before bed. It's not a tool you visit — it's one you live in.

3

A live AI for every module

Fitness, finance, learning, performance — each is backed by its own live AI, not one generic chatbot bolted on top. Ask the fitness assistant about recovery, the finance assistant about a purchase, the learning assistant to explain something twice — each one actually knows that part of your life.

Is my data private?

Yes. Nervexus is a personal system, not a product sold to advertisers or a third party. Everything logged stays yours.

What does it cost?

Nothing — it's a personal project, not a commercial product. There's no subscription, no paywall, no upsell.

Will it remind me about things?

Yes — smart push and email reminders are built into every module, so nothing quietly falls through the cracks during a busy day.

Does it work across every device?

Yes — log in from your phone, tablet, or laptop and everything is exactly where you left it, synced automatically.
