The material

Why cedar shake

Cedar shakes are split from the log rather than sawn, giving each one a rich, uneven texture. Over time the timber weathers from honey brown to a soft silver grey that blends into its surroundings.

Cedar is naturally resistant to rot and insects. We use treated shakes where fire regulations require it, and advise on ventilation and care so the roof reaches its full life.

01

Much lighter than slate or clay, ideal for timber frames and extensions.

How we install it

Done properly, once

  1. 01

    Confirm fire and planning requirements, and specify treated shakes where needed.

  2. 02

    Fit a ventilated, breathable build-up so the timber can dry between showers.

  3. 03

    Lay shakes with careful spacing and staggered joints, fixed with stainless steel.

  4. 04

    Inspect every course and detail, then sign off with care advice and a full walkthrough.
