Our Process

From Quarry to Roofline

A straightforward journey from the first conversation to the final inspection.

Step 01

Consultation

We walk the site, discuss materials and scope, and provide a clear, itemized quote before anything is ordered.

Duration: 60 to 90 minutes
Step 02

Sourcing

Slate and stone are hand-selected from our partner quarries and tracked all the way to your address.

Duration: 3 to 5 days
Step 03

Installation

A licensed, in-house crew installs to spec, with a site lead supervising every stage of the build.

Duration: 1 to 3 weeks
Step 04

Inspection

A full walkthrough and sign-off, backed by a 25-year workmanship warranty on every install.

Duration: 45 to 60 minutes
