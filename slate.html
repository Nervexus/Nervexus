Our Process

From Quarry to Roofline

A straightforward journey from the first conversation to the final inspection.

Consultation

Duration: 60 to 90 minutes
Step 02

Sourcing

Duration: 3 to 5 days
Step 03

Installation

A licensed, in-house crew installs to spec, with a site lead supervising every stage of the build.

Duration: 1 to 3 weeks

Inspection

Duration: 45 to 60 minutes

Questions

Good to know

How do I book a consultation?+

Fill in the booking form or call us on +44 20 7946 0192. We will call you back and arrange a time to visit that suits you.

What happens at the consultation?+

We walk the site, discuss materials and scope, and give you a clear, itemised quote before anything is ordered. It usually takes 60 to 90 minutes.

How long does a new roof take?+

Most installations take one to three weeks, depending on the size of the roof and the material. A site lead supervises every stage of the build.

Is the work guaranteed?+

Yes. Every install is backed by a 25-year workmanship warranty, and the job is signed off with a full walkthrough at the end.

Which roofing materials do you work with?+

Natural slate, standing seam metal, clay tile and cedar shake. If you are not sure which suits your home, we will advise you at the consultation.
