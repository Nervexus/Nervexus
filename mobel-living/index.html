Featured — Fjord Sectional

01

Form

Six modules, one silhouette. Fjord rearranges itself around however the room is actually used — reading corner one month, a seat for twelve the next.

02

Material

Undyed wool bouclé over a kiln-dried hardwood frame. The cover is woven, not printed — so the colour doesn't sit on top of the fibre, it runs through it.

03

Comfort

A 42° seat back and a down-wrapped foam core, tuned to hold its shape for years rather than feel soft for the first six months.

Four Materials

01

Textile

Undyed wool bouclé, woven in small batches so every bolt reads slightly differently — the way a natural fibre should.

02

Wood

European oak, air-dried for eighteen months before it ever reaches a saw. No veneer, no laminate — the grain you see is the grain you get.

03

Stone

Quarried travertine, cut thick and left with a honed — not polished — face, so light sits in it rather than bounces off it.

04

Craft

Every piece passes through the same six hands from frame to finish. We keep the studio small enough that it still can.

Our Story

Built slowly, on purpose

MØBEL started in a single workshop with four people and a conviction that furniture should outlast the room it's bought for. Ten years on, that's still the only brief we work to.
